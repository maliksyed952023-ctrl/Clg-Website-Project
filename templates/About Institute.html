About Institute

Pursuit For Excellence…Government Polytechnic Aurangabad is one of the premier technical institute in the state of Maharashtra, established in the year 1955. The Government of Maharashtra has granted academic autonomy to this institute in 1994. The institute is a recipient of Best Polytechnic Award in 1996-97. The institute has developed strong links with various industries, academic & research institutes. The institution is an enthusiastic aspirant for total autonomous status, with the intension of catering to the needs of industries & society in a better way. Outcome based curriculum (OBE) is introduced to provide more skills in diploma technicians since academic year 2017-18. The institute is equipped with an excellent infrastructure that includes a huge green campus,laboratories, workshop, computer centers, library, sports facilities, play ground, conference and gymkhana hall, staff development cell, examination cell and training & placement cell..

Vision

To be the internationally accredited institute that contributes in the development of competent professionals & entrepreneurs on the platform of technology based systems, blended learning through highly qualified and trained staff.

Mission

To educate and train globally competent individuals, professionals, technicians and skilled human resources through world class curriculum, student centric academic system, team of committed, trained faculty and staff contributing to the student’s successful employment and entrepreneurship with a spirit of patriotism and concern for the environment.

Long-Term Objectives

  • The institute will establish itself into a fully autonomous institute.
  • The institute will grow as a deemed university status in the area of technical management, human resource development entrepreneurship development in sequential growth of technician program.
  • The institute will strengthen its lead institute status and support , guide and network other technical institutes to make them grow, sustain and become leaders
  • This institute will develop disadvantaged groups in the community into knowledge and value added society who can harness the resources.
  • To develop programs to cater to the needs of masses who are not able to attend the classes physically (virtual teaching)
  • To develop web laboratories to have the networking with other technical institute in the worlds for sharing knowledge and resources.
  • To develop training centers at institute to cater the need of industry persons at all levels
  • To develop and strengthen industry institute participation directly in teaching , learning process and financial aspects of the institute.

Milestones

• Established in the year 1955 with three branches Civil Engg., Mechanical Engg. and Electrical Engg. each of 60 intake and having intake qualification Secondary School Certificate (SSC).

• Diploma in Electronics and Radio Engineering was introduced from the year 1978 having intake qualification Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSC).

• Part time diploma programmes in Civil and Mechanical Engineering having intake of 30 were introduced in the year 1976.

• Diploma in Industrial Electronics having intake 30 was introduced from the academic year 1982-83.

• Diploma in Dress Designing and Garment Manufacturing (DDGM) was introduced from the academic Year 1992-93.

• Academic Autonomy is granted to the institute by Government of Maharashtra in the year 1994.

• Institute started developing its own curriculum from the year 1994.

• Institute has started evaluation and certification from the year 1994.

• First batch of Autonomous institute has come out in the year 1997.

• Institute got Best Polytechnic Award from Govt. of Maharashtra in the year 1996.

• Diploma in Electronics and Radio Engineering was discontinued from the academic year.

• Diploma in Information Technology was introduced in the year 2001.

• Four Programmes (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical and Industrial Electronics) of the Institute were accredited by National Board of Accreditation in the year 2003. Institute was the first amongst all institutes in the region and one of the few institutes in Maharashtra to get accreditation.

• Institute has organized National Conference on Emerging Trends in Engineering Education.

• Institute has organized IT literacy campaign in 149 villages of Marathwada region. Entire campaign was organized in collaboration and funds of Marathwada Statutory Development Board.

• From the academic year 2001-02 Diploma in Industrial Electronics was discontinued and Diploma in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering was introduced with the intake of 60.

• Institute received ISTE Narsee-Monjee Award: “The Polytechnic having Best Overall Performance – 2006”.

• Intake of Diploma in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering was increased to 120 from the academic year 2007-2008.

• Diploma in Computer Engineering having intake 30 was introduced from the academic year 2009-2010.

• Diploma in Automobile Engineering having intake 30 was introduced from the academic year 2009-2010.

• Institute received Engg. Education Foundation’s Award: “The Polytechnic having Best Overall Performance – 2010”.

• Institute has started community college from the year 2014.
