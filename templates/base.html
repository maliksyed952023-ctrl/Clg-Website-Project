About Government Polytechnic

Government Polytechnic Aurangabad is one of the premier technical institutes in the state of Maharashtra, established in the year 1955. The Government of Maharashtra has granted academic autonomy to this institute in 1994.

The institute is a recipient of Best Polytechnic Award in 1996-97. The institute has developed strong links with various industries, academic & research institutes, with the intention of catering to the needs of industries & society in a better way.

The institute is equipped with an excellent infrastructure including a huge green campus, laboratories, workshop, computer centers, library, sports facilities, gymkhana hall, staff development cell, examination cell and training & placement cell.
