About Examination Cell

EXAMINATION CELL

Examination Cell has been functioning ever since Institute has been granted Academic Autonomy. Controller of Examination will work as coordinator and executive of the cell along with required number of supporting staff. This Cell maintains students' records regarding examination. Exam Cell will conduct examinations, declare results & perform all other relevant functions.
