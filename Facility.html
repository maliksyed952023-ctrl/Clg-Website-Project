The library of Government Polytechnic, Aurangabad provides a wide collection of technical books, reference materials, journals and digital resources to support the academic needs of students and faculty.

Gymkhana

Government Polytechnic, Aurangabad (famous as GPA) was established in 1955 with genuine intention to provide quality education to all the classes of society. To keep pace with the academic development of the students, institute strives for the all round development of them through various curricular and extra-curricular activities. Gymkhana is the regulatory body governing all sports, cultural and extra- curricular activities at GPA. Gymkhana plays pivotal role in overall development of students. It gives platform students to showcase their talent in sports and cultural event. Gymkhana activities help to improve skills like dedication, involvement, balance and leadership. Students of all departments take active participation in various activities conducted through Gymkhana.
